Wolof greetings and basic phrases

Greetings matter a lot in Senegal. Taking time to greet properly is a sign of respect, and it is the fastest way to be welcomed. Here are the essential ones.

Common greetings

WolofEnglish
Salaam aleekumHello (peace be upon you)
Maleekum salaamReply to the greeting
Nanga def ?How are you?
Maa ngi fi rekkI'm fine, I'm just here
Jàmm nga fanaan ?Did you sleep in peace?
Jàmm rekkPeace only (I'm well)
JërëjëfThank you
Baal maSorry / Excuse me
WaawYes
DéedéetNo
Ba beneen yoonSee you next time
Ba subaSee you tomorrow

Tips

  • Greet first, then ask for what you need.
  • Shake hands and ask about the person and their family.
  • Reply to a greeting with the matching answer, for example Salaam aleekum with Maleekum salaam.
