How numbers are built

From six to nine, Wolof adds to five: juróom benn is five plus one, six. Learn 1 to 5 and 10, and you already know most of the others.

Use them at the market

Ask Ñaata la ? (how much is it?) and listen for the number. Practise pronunciation with real audio in the Diisoo app.
