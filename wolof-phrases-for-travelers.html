Wolof phrases for travelers

Visiting Senegal? These short phrases help in markets, taxis, restaurants and hotels, and people will appreciate your effort.

Useful phrases

WolofEnglish
Ñaata la ?How much is it?
Dafa seerIt is too expensive
Wàññi ko tuutiLower the price a little
Fan la ... nekk ?Where is ... ?
DéggumaI don't understand
Ndax dégg nga Àngale ?Do you speak English?
Dama bëgg ndoxI want water
Dama xiifI am hungry
Dama marI am thirsty
Jërëjëf ak terangaThank you and thanks for your hospitality
Baal maExcuse me / Sorry

Good to know

  • French is the official language, but Wolof is what most people speak every day.
  • Prices at markets and with taxis are often negotiated.
  • A smile and a greeting go a long way.
