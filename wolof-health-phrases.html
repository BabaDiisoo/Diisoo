Wolof health phrases: talk about your body and pain

Knowing how to say where it hurts helps you be understood at the doctor or the pharmacy. Here are the essential Wolof words and phrases.

Body and health

WolofEnglish
YaramBody
BoppHead
LoxoHand
TankFoot
FeebarIllness
FajuTo heal
Feebar naaI am sick
Sama bopp dafay mettiMy head hurts
Soxla naa gis doktoorI need to see a doctor
Dama gën a baax léegiI feel better now
Lu la metti ?What hurts?
Fan la doktoor bi ?Where is the doctor?

Tips

  • Greet the person first, then explain the problem.
  • If someone asks Lu la metti ? (what hurts?), point to where it hurts.
  • If in doubt, ask: Fan la doktoor bi ?
