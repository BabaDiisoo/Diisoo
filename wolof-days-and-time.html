Wolof days of the week and time

To set a meeting or talk about your work or trip, you need the days of the week and a few time words. Here they are, with ready-to-use phrases.

Days of the week

WolofEnglish
AltineMonday
TalaataTuesday
ÀllarbaWednesday
AlxamisThursday
ÀjjumaFriday
GaawuSaturday
DibéerSunday

Useful phrases

WolofEnglish
Ban waxtu la ?What time is it?
Ba ëllëgSee you tomorrow
Dinaa ñëw ëllëgI will come tomorrow
Sonn naa teyI am tired today
Démb, dama am liggéey bu bariYesterday, I was busy
Altine ci subaMonday morning

A tip to remember the days

Start with Altine (Monday), Àjjuma (Friday) and Dibéer (Sunday), the three most useful days, then add the others one by one. Link each day to an activity in your week.
