Learn Wolof online

Wolof is the most widely spoken language in Senegal and is understood by most people in Dakar, Thiès and across the country. A few phrases open doors, make people smile and change how you are welcomed. Here is how to start.

Start with these 6 words

WolofEnglish
Salaam aleekumHello
Nanga def ?How are you?
Maa ngi fi rekkI'm fine
JërëjëfThank you
WaawYes
DéedéetNo

How to read Wolof letters

  • x : like the 'ch' in Scottish 'loch'
  • ñ : like 'ny' in 'canyon'
  • ë : a short, neutral vowel, close to the 'u' in 'but'
  • aa, ee, ii, oo, uu : doubled vowels are held longer
  • ŋ : like the 'ng' in 'sing'

Why learn Wolof?

Wolof is the shared language of Senegalese cities, markets and families. Speaking even a little shows respect and opens real conversations, whether you travel, work, volunteer or want to reconnect with family roots.
