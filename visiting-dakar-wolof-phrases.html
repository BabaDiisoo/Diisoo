Visiting Dakar? 15 Wolof phrases in 5 minutes

A few words of Wolof change a stay in Dakar: people smile, prices feel fairer and the welcome is warmer. Here are the 15 most useful phrases, grouped by situation.

Greet and say thanks

WolofEnglish
Salaam aleekumHello
Nanga def ?How are you?
JërëjëfThank you
Jërëjëf ak terangaThank you for your hospitality

Make yourself understood

WolofEnglish
DéggumaI don't understand
Ndax dégg nga Àngale ?Do you speak English?
Baal maExcuse me / Sorry

Get around and pay

WolofEnglish
Fan la ... nekk ?Where is ... ?
Ñaata la ?How much is it?
Wàññi ko tuutiLower the price a little
Ndax nangu ngeen Wave ak Orange Money ?Do you accept Wave and Orange Money?

Eat and drink

WolofEnglish
Dama xiifI am hungry
Dama bëgg ndoxI want water

If you need health help

WolofEnglish
Soxla naa gis doktoorI need to see a doctor
Fan la doktoor bi ?Where is the doctor?

A good habit: greet first

In Senegal, you always greet before asking a question or negotiating a price. Say Salaam aleekum, ask Nanga def ?, then make your request. A "Jërëjëf" at the end always goes down well.
