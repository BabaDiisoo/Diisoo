Wolof food and restaurant phrases

In Senegal, a meal is a moment to share. Here are the Wolof words and phrases to order, say you are hungry or compliment the dish.

Food and meals

WolofEnglish
CeebRice
JënFish
YàppMeat
MburuBread
MeewMilk
NeexTasty
Dama xiifI am hungry
Ñam bi neex naThis food is delicious
Bëgg naa lekk ceeb ak jënI want to eat rice and fish
Nanu lekk àndLet's eat together
Ndox miThe water

Tips

  • Say Ñam bi neex na to compliment the meal: it is always appreciated.
  • Invite others to eat together with Nanu lekk ànd.
  • Listen to the phrase first, then repeat it out loud.
