Wolof at the market: prices and bargaining

Shopping in a Senegalese market is easier and more fun when you speak a few words of Wolof. These phrases help you ask the price, bargain with a smile and pay.

Phrases for buying

WolofEnglish
Ñaata la tamaate yi ?How much are the tomatoes?
Dafa seer loolThat is too expensive
Mën nga wàññi njëg li ?Can you make it cheaper?
Mën nga ma wàññil ci njëg li ?Could you give me a discount?
Dafa yombIt is cheap
Dama bëgg jënd ceebI would like to buy some rice
Ndax nangu ngeen Wave ak Orange Money ?Do you accept Wave and Orange Money?

Good to know

  • Prices at markets are often negotiable, so a polite request to lower the price is normal.
  • Start with a greeting before you ask the price. It changes the mood of the whole conversation.
  • Wave and Orange Money are widely used in Senegal, but small sellers may still prefer cash.
