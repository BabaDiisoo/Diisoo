Speak Wolof with your family

Going home to Senegal, or talking with relatives by phone? A few Wolof phrases show respect and bring you closer to parents and grandparents. Start with these.

Phrases for family visits

WolofEnglish
Salaam maleekumPeace be upon you (greeting)
Maleekum salaamAnd upon you peace (reply)
Nanga def ?How are you?
Maa ngi fi rekkI am fine (I am just here)
Naka waa kër ga ?How is the family?
Naka sa yaay ?How is your mother?
Naka sa baay ?How is your father?
Ñibbsi naaI am back home
Maa ngi jàng wolofI am learning Wolof
JërëjëfThank you
Ba beneen yoonUntil next time

Key words

WolofEnglish

Magelder

Good to know

  • Greet first, and ask about the family before anything else. It is a sign of respect.
  • Elders deserve extra respect. A warm greeting matters more than perfect grammar.
  • Do not worry about mistakes: relatives will be happy that you try and will gladly correct you.
