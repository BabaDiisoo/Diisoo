Privacy and data protection

Last updated: October 16, 2026.

Who is responsible

Diisoo is a product of ABC-GLOBAL (Africain Business Consulting), Thiès, Senegal. We will never ask you for a password, a code received by SMS or a payment by message to use Diisoo.

Data we collect

  • Your first name, to welcome you in the app.
  • Your WhatsApp number, only if you enter it yourself (the "Add your WhatsApp" card). It is optional.
  • If you sign in with Google: your name and e-mail address, provided by Google.
  • Your progress in the lessons, saved on your phone.

What we use it for

  • To send you revision reminders and follow your progress.
  • To inform you about Diisoo news and ABC-GLOBAL services.

Who can see your number

Only the ABC-GLOBAL team can see your number. It is stored in our database, with no public access. We do not sell your data.

Your rights

You can ask at any time to see, correct or delete your data, or to stop receiving messages. Write to us with your number: abcglobal33@gmail.com or WhatsApp +221 77 288 21 09.

These rights are provided by Senegalese law no. 2008-12 of January 25, 2008 on the protection of personal data.
